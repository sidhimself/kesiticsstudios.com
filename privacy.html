Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Kesitics Studios LLC ("we", "us") handles information when you use our website and our mobile apps.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • App data. Each of our apps describes the data it uses in its App Store privacy label. Wherever possible, data stays on your device.
  • Purchases. Apple processes all in-app purchases and subscriptions. We never receive your payment card details.

How we use information

We use information only to provide and improve our apps, answer support requests, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Our apps may use trusted service providers, such as analytics or crash reporting, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. These services are listed in each app's App Store privacy details.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete any information we hold about you by emailing us.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
